# Scheduling Best Practices for Knowledge Workers

## Time Blocking Strategies

### The Maker's Schedule
- Reserve 2-4 hour uninterrupted blocks for deep work
- Place meetings in the afternoon when possible
- Batch similar meetings together

### Energy Management
- **9:00-11:30**: Peak cognitive hours → Strategic/creative work
- **11:30-13:00**: Pre-lunch → Collaborative meetings
- **13:00-14:00**: Post-lunch dip → Administrative tasks
- **14:00-16:00**: Second wind → Meetings and calls
- **16:00-17:30**: Wind-down → Email, planning next day

### Buffer Time Rules
- 15 min buffer between back-to-back meetings
- 30 min prep time before important meetings
- 15 min debrief after strategic meetings

## Meeting Hygiene
- Default to 25 min (not 30) or 50 min (not 60)
- Every meeting needs a clear agenda
- Meetings with >6 people should be presentations, not discussions
- No-meeting days: designate 1-2 days/week as meeting-free
